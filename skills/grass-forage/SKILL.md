# RB209 Section 3: Grass and Forage Crops

Use this skill for nutrient recommendations for managed grassland: silage, grazing, hay, and establishment.
Always consult the **nutrient-principles** skill for soil index classification, SNS lookup, and conversion factors.

---

## Grass Growth Class

Classify grassland into growth classes before making nitrogen recommendations:

| Class | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Low-yielding grassland; poor soils, limited water availability |
| 2 | Moderate-yielding; average conditions |
| 3 | High-yielding; good soils, adequate moisture, high management |
| 4 | Very high-yielding; irrigated or very high rainfall, intensive management |

Growth class is determined by:
- Soil type and water-holding capacity
- Annual rainfall (>850 mm generally class 3–4)
- Previous management and production history

### SNS Adjustments for Grass History

Adjust SNS Index from field assessment lookup:
- **Grass previously cut (high N residues)**: +1 SNS index
- **Grazed grassland only**: no adjustment
- **Ploughed grass ley < 3 years**: add 30 kg N/ha to arable SNS value
- **Ploughed grass ley 3–5 years**: add 30–60 kg N/ha
- **Ploughed grass ley > 5 years**: add 60+ kg N/ha

## Nitrogen Recommendations

### Silage Systems (kg N/ha/year)

| Target DM yield (t/ha) | Growth Class 1 | Growth Class 2 | Growth Class 3 | Growth Class 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 70 | 90 | — | — |
| 7 | 100 | 130 | 160 | — |
| 9 | 130 | 170 | 200 | — |
| 11 | — | 210 | 240 | 270 |
| 13 | — | — | 280 | 300 |
| 15+ | — | — | — | 310 |

Split nitrogen between cuts:
- **1st cut**: apply 40–60 kg N/ha in February/March (earlier on growth class 3–4)
- **After each cut**: apply 40–80 kg N/ha promptly after harvest
- Final application no later than **end of August** (high rainfall areas) or **mid-September** (low rainfall)

### Grazing Systems (kg N/ha/year)

| Target yield | Growth Class 1 | Growth Class 2 | Growth Class 3 | Growth Class 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 0–80 | 50–120 | 100–180 | — |
| Medium | 80–150 | 120–190 | 180–250 | 220–280 |
| High | — | 190–250 | 250–300 | 280–350 |

Apply in **4–8 split dressings** from February/March to August/September.
First application: **25–40 kg N/ha** in late February/early March.

### Hay Systems (kg N/ha/year)

| Target DM yield (t/ha) | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 3 | 50–80 |
| 5 | 80–120 |
| 7 | 100–150 |

Apply all nitrogen in a single dressing in February/March, 6–8 weeks before cutting.

### Grass Establishment (new leys)

| Establishment method | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Spring sown | 25–50 kg N/ha as seedbed fertiliser |
| Late summer / autumn sown | 0–25 kg N/ha; avoid late applications |
| Overseeding into existing sward | 0 kg N/ha until establishment confirmed |

## Grass-Clover Swards

Where clover content exceeds **30%** of the sward:
- Reduce total nitrogen fertiliser by **50–100 kg N/ha/year**
- At very high clover content (>50%), **no nitrogen fertiliser** may be needed
- Avoid high N applications which suppress clover growth
- Clover fixes 100–200 kg N/ha/year under good growing conditions

## Potash (K₂O) Recommendations for Grass

### Silage Systems

Recommendations (kg K₂O/ha/year) by soil K Index:

| System | Index 0 | Index 1 | Index 2- | Index 2+ | Index 3 | Index 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silage (3+ cuts) | 180 | 140 | 100 | 80 | 50 | 0 |
| Silage (1–2 cuts) | 140 | 110 | 80 | 60 | 30 | 0 |

**Important**: To minimise luxury K uptake, limit spring application to **80–90 kg K₂O/ha** for 1st cut silage. Apply remaining K after harvest.

### Grazing and Hay Systems

| System | Index 0 | Index 1 | Index 2- | Index 2+ | Index 3 | Index 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grazing | 60 | 45 | 30 | 20 | 0 | 0 |
| Hay | 90 | 70 | 50 | 35 | 20 | 0 |

Under grazing, potassium is largely recycled via dung and urine; hence lower fertiliser K requirements.

## Sulphur Recommendations for Grass

Sulphur-deficiency risk on grassland:
- Sandy or light-textured soils
- High-rainfall areas
- Soils low in organic matter

| Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| HIGH | 25–50 kg SO₃/ha/year |
| MODERATE | 25 kg SO₃/ha/year |
| LOW | 0 kg SO₃/ha/year |

Apply in early spring with the first nitrogen dressing. Silage crops are more at risk than grazed swards because SO₃ is removed at harvest.

---

## Key Application Guidance

1. **First nitrogen in early spring**: Apply as soon as soil conditions and weather allow; typically February (south) to March (north)
2. **Final nitrogen application**: End of August for high-rainfall/northern areas; mid-September elsewhere
3. **Silage aftermath**: Apply nitrogen and potassium promptly after each cut (within 48 hours if possible)
4. **Avoid surface application in wet conditions**: Risk of runoff into watercourses (Farming Rules for Water)
5. **NVZ closed periods**: Check local NVZ rules for closed periods for nitrogen fertilisers on grassland
6. **Organic manures**: Slurry and digestate are excellent nutrient sources for grass; always deduct their available nutrient contributions before calculating fertiliser need
7. **Soil testing**: Sample every 3–5 years; sample under grazing when sward is short and soil not poached

## Using the RB209 API for Grassland Recommendations

For live, authoritative recommendations rather than static table look-ups, use the RB209 API tools:

1. Call **rb209_get_soil_types** to confirm the SoilTypeID for the field.
2. Build the `fieldData` object with:
   - `Field.FieldType = 2` (Grassland)
   - `Field.Grassland` containing SNSID, GrassGrowthClassID, YieldTypeID, SequenceID, and GrasslandSequence (position, crop material ID, and yield per cut)
   - `Field.Soil` with SoilTypeID and a SoilAnalyses entry containing pH, SNS index, P index, K index, Mg index
3. Call **rb209_get_recommendations** with the assembled fieldData.

The response will include `Recommendations` (kg/ha by nutrient and cut sequence), `NutrientsSupplied` (from organic materials if provided), and `AdviceNotes`.
