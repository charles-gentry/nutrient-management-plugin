# RB209 Section 4: Arable Crops

Use this skill for nutrient recommendations for cereals, oilseeds, sugar beet, peas, and beans.

## Nitrogen Recommendations

### Winter Wheat

Recommendations (kg N/ha) by SNS Index and soil category:

| SNS Index | Soil cat. 1–2 (shallow/sand) | Soil cat. 3–4 (medium) | Soil cat. 5 (clay) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 220 | 250* | 220 |
| 1 | 200 | 220 | 200 |
| 2 | 180 | 200 | 180 |
| 3 | 160 | 180 | 160 |
| 4 | 130 | 150 | 130 |
| 5 | 100 | 110 | 100 |
| 6 | 60 | 70 | 60 |

\* Yield adjustments permitted: +10 kg N/ha per tonne above 8 t/ha target yield (max adjustment 40 kg N/ha).

Apply in **2–3 splits**: first at GS25–30, second at GS31, third (if used) at GS37–39.

### Winter Barley

Reduce winter wheat rates by approximately 40 kg N/ha.

### Sugar Beet

| SNS Index | Recommendation (kg N/ha) |
|---|---|
| 0 | 150 |
| 1 | 120 |
| 2 | 100 |
| 3 | 70 |
| 4 | 40 |
| 5–6 | 0 |

Apply all N before or at drilling. Split application rarely beneficial.

### Peas and Beans

Peas and beans **fix their own nitrogen** via rhizobia — generally **no nitrogen fertiliser** is required.
Exception: small starter dose (up to 30 kg N/ha) on low-N soils for early establishment only.

## Sulphur Recommendations

Sulphur deficiency risk factors:
- Light-textured soils (sands, sandy loams)
- High-rainfall areas
- Low organic matter soils

### Risk Categories

| Risk | Soil type / rainfall | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH | Sandy, high rainfall | 25–50 kg SO₃/ha |
| MODERATE | Sandy loam, moderate rainfall | 25 kg SO₃/ha |
| LOW | Clay or silt soils | 0 kg SO₃/ha |

Apply between **February and April**. Cereals and oilseed rape are the most sulphur-responsive crops.

## Micronutrient Guidance

### Cereals — Manganese (Mn)
- Risk soils: sandy, high pH, high organic matter
- Deficiency symptoms: inter-veinal chlorosis on young leaves
- Soil threshold: <5 mg/L EDTA-Mn; leaf threshold: <20 mg/kg
- Apply foliar Mn sulphate (2–3 kg Mn/ha in 200 L water) at early tillering

### Cereals — Copper (Cu)
- Risk soils: peaty, organic, alkaline sandy soils
- Soil threshold: <1 mg/L EDTA-Cu; leaf threshold: <5 mg/kg
- Apply foliar Cu (100–200 g Cu/ha) if deficiency confirmed

### Oilseed Rape — Boron (B)
- Risk soils: sandy, acid, high organic matter
- Leaf threshold: <20 mg/kg
- Apply 0.5–1.0 kg B/ha as foliar spray at rosette to early stem extension

### Oilseed Rape — Molybdenum (Mo)
- Risk: acid soils (pH <6.0)
- Apply lime first to raise pH; if needed, 70–100 g Mo/ha as foliar spray

## Key Application Guidance

1. **Nitrogen**: Apply in splits aligned to crop growth stages; avoid application in waterlogged conditions
2. **Phosphate and potash**: Can be applied in autumn or spring, before drilling or as seedbed fertiliser
3. **Sulphur**: Early spring application (Feb–Apr) before active crop growth
4. **Straw management**: Straw incorporation reduces potash requirements; straw removal increases them significantly
5. **Organic materials**: Always calculate and deduct crop-available nutrients from manures/slurries before calculating fertiliser need
